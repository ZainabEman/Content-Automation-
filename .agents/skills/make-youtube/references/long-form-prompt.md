# Long-form YouTube script (about 12 minutes)

Write a news analysis in spoken German for Roman alone (no host), about 1,600-1,800 words
(config/youtube.json formats.long.targetWords). One script per style (A, B, C); the styles differ
in hook, story, delivery and structure.

- 5-10 topics, 2-3 minutes each by importance; adjust so the total stays at about 12 minutes.
- Structure: a strong hook (a real, sourced statistic, risk or finding; not sensational; say what
  viewers can do) → topic segments (the news → why it matters → analysis and implications), with
  clear spoken transitions → a short conclusion.
- Balanced: risks and positive developments.
- Only verified facts from research.txt (fact IDs [F#]); numbers exactly as written. The IDs,
  sources and URLs stay out of the teleprompter file; the storyboard carries the references.
- Address the viewer with "Sie". Short sentences, the verb early, numbers written as spoken.
- Never imply Roman experienced or witnessed something he did not; no invented story, number,
  quotation or event.
